# Release 98 — UI/UX Phase 2: Members & Roles + Member 360

## Scope
Visual-only continuation of the approved ScoutSVM design direction. Existing routes, Supabase operations, role assignment logic, filters, and permissions were preserved.

## Admin Members
- Brand-aligned page hero and scout-year badge.
- Navy/orange/gold KPI treatment.
- Clearer Chef / Equipier creation tabs.
- Cleaner form fields and focus states.
- Dedicated filter surface for roster management.
- Improved member list hierarchy with generated initial avatars, status and role-count pills.
- Strong selected-row state and clearer member-detail panel.
- Sticky detail panel on desktop; normal stacked flow on mobile.
- Improved Member 360 action and current-role editor styling.

## Member 360
- Same visual identity as Members management.
- Stronger profile photo treatment and fallback avatar.
- Cleaner information cards and responsibilities surface.
- Better mobile stacking and action sizing.

## Not changed
- No Supabase queries or RPCs changed.
- No permissions or RLS changed.
- No routes changed.
- No member/role workflow changed.
- No database migration.
